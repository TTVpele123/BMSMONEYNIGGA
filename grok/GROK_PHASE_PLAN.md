BMSMONEYNIGGA only. Deal OS is not the active architecture.

## Phase 1 — WhatsApp + match + email dry-run (NOW)

**Prereqs:** `./start.sh`, dry_run, imported buyers optional.  
**Agents:** WHATSAPP_SCANNER required. BUYER_RESEARCHER + OPPORTUNITY_RESEARCHER optional after proof.  
**Tools:** WhatsApp Web, localhost ingest/snapshot/metrics/tick.  
**Permissions:** Browser on Oliver chat; no Gmail send.  
**Risks:** Chat screenshots; inventing facts; burning quota on research first.  
**Success:** Real lot + original photos + matches + dry_run attempts + `live_sends=0`.  
**Do not enable:** live email, forms, IG, LI, marketplaces, mega-agent.

## Phase 2 — Research + website forms (prepare)

**Prereqs:** Phase 1 success on a real Oliver drop.  
**Agents:** BUYER_RESEARCHER routine; FORM_OPERATOR created in **prepare/defer** mode only.  
**Tools:** Public sites; form URL from endpoints; still no submit unless Bailey enables later.  
**Permissions:** `executable: false` on forms.  
**Risks:** CAPTCHA farming; submitting vendor apps with invented docs.  
**Success:** Evidenced endpoints; form opportunities stay `deferred`; more qualified matches.  
**Do not enable:** IG/LI DMs; live form submit; guessed emails.

**Prereqs:** Official or human-approved accounts; written rate limits; Phase 2 form prep proven.
